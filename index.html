Frequently Asked Questions

Have another question? Call us at (231) 943-6512.

How many sessions will I need?

The standard protocol is four 30-minute sessions over about two weeks, spaced two to three days apart. Your consultation determines whether additional sessions would help.

Is there any downtime?

No. You can return to all normal activities immediately after your session.

Does it hurt?

Patients describe it as an intense workout, with strong muscle contractions and a warm sensation from the RF. Intensity is raised gradually, and no anesthesia or numbing is needed.

Who is a good candidate?

Adults within about 20 to 30 pounds of their ideal body weight, with stubborn fat in the abdomen, arms, thighs, buttocks, or calves, in good general health, with realistic expectations. It is a body-contouring treatment, not a weight-loss treatment. It is not recommended with pacemakers, implanted defibrillators or neurostimulators, metal implants in the treatment area, or during pregnancy.

Can I do it while on a GLP-1 medication?

Yes. A 2025 chart review found that patients who combined a GLP-1 with HIFEM+RF treatment gained muscle on average, while patients on a GLP-1 alone lost muscle. We track your progress with InBody scans.

When will I see results?

Most patients notice improved tone within 2 to 4 weeks. Peak results appear about 3 months after your final session.

How long do results last?

Typically 6 to 12 months or longer, depending on lifestyle. Maintenance sessions every three to six months help preserve your results.

How much does it cost?

Sessions typically run $750 to $1,000, and a four-session series about $3,000 to $4,000. Book through this page to take $250 off your series. Cherry financing is available.

Is it covered by insurance?

No. Cosmetic treatment is not covered by insurance. Cherry financing is available.
